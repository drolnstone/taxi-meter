WHAT CHANGED (features taken from the minibus and ushers apps)
- Vehicle tab: a daily vehicle check (Fine / Advisory / Defect on 28 items,
  critical items marked) that goes to a new "Vehicle Checks" tab on the sheet.
- Renewal dates for plate, badge, insurance, MOT, DVLA licence and meter test,
  with reminders on the meter screen 60, 30 and 7 days out.
- Before a trip: asked if plate, badge, insurance or MOT has run out, or if
  today's check found a critical defect. Going ahead is noted on the Log tab.
  Asked once a day if no check has been done.
- Save a receipt as a PDF (Trip complete screen, and any past trip).
- Earnings statement PDF for this week, last week, this month, last month or
  the tax year to date (Earnings tab).
- Settings shows "App 1.8.0 · Sheet 1.1.0".
- tests/run-tests.mjs and a Tests workflow on GitHub.

DEPLOY
1. Paste Code.gs into the Apps Script editor, save, then
   Deploy → Manage deployments → edit → New version.
2. Publish index.html, sw.js, vehicle.js, pdf.js and the vendor/ folder to the
   same place as before (all in the same folder, vendor/ beside index.html).
3. Reload the spreadsheet. Nothing to migrate: the "Vehicle Checks" tab is
   made by the first check sent.
